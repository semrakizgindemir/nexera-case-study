# Frequently Asked Questions (Sample Content)

> Fictional sample data for the case study. Not real product data.

## How long does it take to learn to code?
It depends on the goal and the pace. A focused beginner can reach a
junior project level in 4 to 8 months with steady practice.

## Do I need a university degree?
A degree can help but is not required. A strong portfolio of finished
projects often matters more for early roles.

## How do I choose what to learn first?
Pick a single direction (backend, frontend, or data), follow one path,
and finish small projects before adding new topics.

## How important is English?
Most documentation and learning resources are in English, so reading
comfort in English is a strong advantage.

## What makes a good portfolio project?
A project that runs, solves a real problem, has a clear README, and
shows the decisions you made and why.
